  persisted key set mirrors Yahoo's response.
- 744 current observations (08:31:50 UTC): 318 `EXPLICIT_STATUS` (NA 218, O
  77, Q 16, P 4, OFS 3), 426 `STATUS_FIELD_MISSING`, and 0 supplied blanks,
  missing observations, stale imports, mapping issues, normalization defects
  or unrecognized tokens.

"verified supplied blank" policy has nothing to apply to, and absence cannot
be proven to mean "no designation". Blank stays UNKNOWN.
