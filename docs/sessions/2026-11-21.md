## Root causes (proven)

1. **Schedule claims were never reconciled with production** (WC-01/02/03,
   P0). The schedule component is the pool-wide midrank percentile of an
   integer game count, added independently of FP/G.
   - **ROS Jackson/Allen:**
     - production −0.310305 (22.59 vs 30.29 FP/G);
     - schedule +1.335916 (66 vs 63 games, the 96.6 vs 9.8 percentile).
     - In Yahoo points over the window the add trails: 66 × 22.6 = 1490.9 vs
       63 × 30.3 = 1908.3 (−417.4).
   - **The same mechanism dropped Luka Dončić:** ROS +0.67 and Next 7
     +0.166.
   - Every Allen-drop pair (12 / 12 / 43) turned negative without schedule.
2. **Prior-season weighting drove the Dunn drop** (WC-04, documented method).
   - DeRozan 24.89 = 0.412 × 16.54 (last 14 days) + 0.588 × 30.73 (2025-26).
   - Dunn 19.26 = 0.375 × 16.92 + 0.625 × 20.66.
   - DeRozan trails on current-season evidence (17.7 vs 23.6).
   - The blend (n/(n+10), next-actions item 6) is unchanged; the case is now
     flagged.
3. **Explanations were static per decision type** (WC-05). They claimed fit,
   role or production that were zero, negative or outside the comparison mask.
4. **Cards mixed production bases** (WC-06, WC-11). The pair card showed raw
   last-14 FP/G while the comparison used blended values. ROS option cards
   showed shrunk "measured" values while the pair used Season Evidence.
5. **OTHER GOOD OPTIONS had no drop, and their group pool excluded supported
   and HOLD players** (WC-07/08). ROS groups featured 1-game,
   insufficient-evidence leftovers with negative nets.
6. **Labels and counts** (WC-09/10/12/13/14/15):
   - five unlabeled label systems;
   - "n/a" for every missing-value cause;
   - lineup gaps counted slot instances ("10 of 7 dates");
   - "unavailable" for watch-only drops;
   - "confirmed available" for unrostered players;
   - tiny nets shown as improvements.

## Fix (model `waiver-decision-integrity-phase7`)

- **Explanation-consistency gate** (`explanation.py`; applied in `enrich` and
  `evaluate_pair`). When the add's production contribution is negative,
  every favorable compensator must hold:
  - schedule iff add games × add FP/G ≥ drop games × drop FP/G (same basis,
    same window);
  - roster fit only with a starting-slot gain;
  - opportunity is never reconcilable.

  A refuted positive pair becomes INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE (verdict DO NOT ADD)
  with the arithmetic in its blocker, on every surface. No weight, threshold
  or player-specific rule changed.
- **Explanations:** each comparison carries:
  - its contributions (they sum to the net within 1e-5);
  - the production basis and blend math for both players;
  - window points;
  - checks (ARITHMETIC, CLAIMS_MEASURED, COMPENSATION, NEAR_TIE);
  - the reconciled net, the statement, the primary reason and tradeoffs:
    FRAGILE, PRIOR-SEASON DRIVEN, "Dropping is permanent", production cost.

  The text names only in-mask positive components.
- **Verdicts:** SUPPORTED ADD, WATCH ONLY, NEAR TIE — NO STRONG PREFERENCE (a
  reconciled |net| below 0.005), DO NOT ADD, CANNOT EVALUATE. Each has a
  distinct style; a featured supported card fails closed unless its verdict
  is SUPPORTED ADD.
- **OTHER GOOD OPTIONS:**
  - every pick carries its verdict and exact best comparison ("Best
    compatible drop", marked "(comparison only)" unless supported), or "No
    supported drop: reason";
  - the pool includes supported, HOLD and WATCH candidates;
  - ordering is verdict first;
  - titles are qualified by the verdict;
  - each card has "Why this drop?" and a "Why listed" reason.
- **Labels:**
  - evidence by dimension (PRODUCTION SAMPLE, CURRENT ROLE, SCHEDULE, SOURCE
    FRESHNESS, TRANSACTION VERDICT);
  - form-window reasons instead of "n/a";
  - lineup gaps in dates, with slot-games separate;
  - roster labels: supported replacement / watch only / keep / cannot
    evaluate;
  - "unrostered in every current league roster";
  - nets at 2 decimals, or 4 when a non-zero net would print as ±0.00.
