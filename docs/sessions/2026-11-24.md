## Findings

1. **Structural.** The Phase 7.1 weekly value is `n/(n+10) × last 14 days + 10/(n+10) × 2025-26`. The window
   never holds ten games, so the prior keeps at least 55.6% of every weekly value all season (all 210 players
   with a prior; 62 of 68 with 15+ current-season games), and 1,440 of 2,715 current-season games (53%) never count.
2. **Decisions.** 63 exact weekly pairs (21 unique) rank an add above a drop who leads on both Last 14 Days and
   the current season; the engine's PRIOR-SEASON DRIVEN flag marks exactly these. **11 are SUPPORTED ADD**,
   including the featured Rest of Week move DeRozan / Dunn (+0.589); none relies on schedule, fit or role.
3. **Backtest** (persisted 2026-27 games, next 7 and 14 days as the observed target, player-cluster bootstrap):
   every alternative beats the control on MAE, rank correlation and pairwise ordering; counting earlier games
   helps; a recency multiplier adds nothing once the prior's share is fixed; accuracy falls steadily as the prior
   grows past about two games. When the control contradicts both current views, the next 7 days side with the
   current views 58% of the time.
4. **Prior size.** Rule fixed before the run (largest K not significantly worse than the best on ordering in both
   horizons, all and fantasy-relevant players): **K = 2**, also the MAE-best everywhere. K = 10 is significantly
   worse. The data cannot separate K = 1–4 for relevant players; decisions are stable for K = 2–5.
5. **Latent Phase 7.1 defect (found, fixed in Phase 8):** the PRIOR-SEASON DRIVEN text formatted the drop's prior
   value unconditionally; a flagged pair whose drop has no prior (e.g. a rookie on the roster) raised `TypeError`
   and failed the board. Not reachable on the current roster.

### Decision: E2 at the Phase-7 position (model `waiver-permanent-drop-phase9`)

- **Rule.** In Rest of Week, Next 7 Days and Next 14 Days, an ADD + DROP whose consistent positive net leans on a
  schedule edge (the add produces less per game and the Phase-7 window check holds) is supported only if
  `window gain >= max(0, drop FP/G - replacement FP/G) x drop games after the window`. Window gain is the
  Phase-7 window Yahoo FP; values are the Phase-8 production basis; replacement level is the best production
  value among players off the manager roster that are confirmed available, identity-resolved, fresh, status-
  verified and sample-supported in the horizon, never below the add; games after the window are the drop's
  persisted remaining regular-season games minus his window games.
- **No fitted threshold:** the boundary is break-even in Yahoo fantasy points. It is monotonic (more window gain,
  a weaker drop, a better replacement or fewer remaining games can only allow) and names no player.
- **Robustness:** identical decisions with replacement = 2nd best, mean of top 3, best by current season or
  position-aware, and with the cost horizon capped at 30, 14, 7 or 3 games after the window. Only a stricter
  replacement (3rd best, 22.18) also blocks DeRozan / Dunn. The largest blocked gain is 6% (Rest of Week), 21%
  (Next 7 Days) and 2% (Next 14 Days) of its cost; the closest case is a WATCH pair against Dillon Brooks (0.6
  FP/G above replacement).
- **Placement:** after the Phase-7 gate and before the sample, Yahoo-status and STREAM role-evidence gates, as the
  explanation gate. A blocked pair is HOLD / DO NOT ADD (INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE / DO NOT ADD while its Yahoo
  status is unverified), never WATCH; WATCH pairs that pass stay WATCH. The optimizer then uses the add's next
  supported drop. Rest of Season Outlook, ADD ONLY, MOVE TO IL THEN ADD, production-led and fit-led pairs are never
  checked.
